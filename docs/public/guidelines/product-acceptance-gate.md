# Product Acceptance Gate

Canonical source guideline for product acceptance and completion evidence.

Acceptance must be tied to user-visible behavior, not only files changed or tasks checked off.
Each accepted scenario needs evidence: test output, browser verification, fixture result, screenshot, or directly runnable artifact.
Known gaps must be classified as deferred, blocked, or residual risk before closeout.
Do not claim completion when required verification is skipped without a stated reason and replacement check.
